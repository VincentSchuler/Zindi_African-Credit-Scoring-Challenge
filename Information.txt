My solution is very straight-forward, and all the code has been carefully commented to be understood more easily.

Most efforts were concentrated towards Feature Engineering, as ~1700 columns were created. However, only 149 of them were used in the final models.

These models are 6 LightGBM and 7 XGBoost, ensembled with a simple average of predictions. However, the ensemble didn't prove very useful in this competition, and I was able to outperform both my public and private score with a single LGBM.

In the end, a special post-processing method was used to gain a considerable score boost. I described it both in my notebook and i the Zindi forum : https://zindi.africa/competitions/african-credit-scoring-challenge/discussions/24427

I consisted in assigning the value "1" to all loans of type 3 (Ghana) that are the very last of each Customer.
